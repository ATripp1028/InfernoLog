## Reporting a Copyright Concern

If you believe InfernoLog contains content that infringes your copyright, contact **contact@infernolog.com**. To help us review your request efficiently, please include:

- A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed (including a link to the original, where applicable).
- The specific location of the material within InfernoLog (a URL or clear description of where it appears).
- Your contact information (email address, and name if you're comfortable providing it), so we can follow up or relay a counter-notice if one is filed.
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
- A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
- A physical or electronic signature. A typed full legal name at the end of your message is sufficient — no special signature format is required.

Incomplete requests may take longer to process while we follow up for missing information.

## What Happens After a Report

We aim to acknowledge valid reports within **5 business days** and resolve them within **14 business days**, though complex cases may take longer.

1. **Review.** We check that the report includes the elements above and evaluate whether the flagged content appears to infringe the claimed copyright.
2. **Removal.** If the claim appears valid, we remove or disable the flagged content and notify the user who posted it, including a copy of the original complaint.
3. **Counter-notice.** If the affected user believes the content was removed in error or misidentification, they may submit a counter-notice to contact@infernolog.com including:
   - Identification of the removed content and where it previously appeared.
   - Their contact information.
   - A statement, under penalty of perjury, that they have a good-faith belief the content was removed by mistake or misidentification.
   - A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of their local federal court (or, if outside the US, an appropriate court of competent jurisdiction) and that they will accept service of process from the original claimant.
   - A physical or electronic signature (a typed full name is sufficient).
4. **Resolution.** If we receive a valid counter-notice and do not hear back from the original claimant within **10 business days**, we may restore the content. If the claimant confirms they've pursued further action, the content stays down pending that outcome.
